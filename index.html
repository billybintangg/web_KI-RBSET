About The Event

Driving the Acceleration of International Clean Energy Transition

KI–RBSET (Renewable Energy System Design and Construction Competition) is an international innovation event specifically designed to facilitate creative ideas, prototypes, and future energy system designs by university students worldwide.

Through this competition, participants are invited to provide applicable solutions to energy challenges, improve the efficiency of renewable energy conversion systems, and support Indonesia's target towards Net Zero Emissions.

Solution Innovation

Developing highly effective scientific ideas capable of answering real challenges in the era of digital transformation.

International Recognition

The best works will be assessed directly by a jury of experts and have the opportunity to be published in scientific proceedings.

Collaboration Trail

Expanding academic and professional networks among students, academics, and industry practitioners across Indonesia.

ASSESSMENT CRITERIA

Competition Assessment Criteria (100 Points)

Assessment is conducted objectively and transparently by an expert jury based on these two main criteria.

Scientific Paper

Assessment Weight: 100%

100 Total Points
Criteria Weight Assessment Standard
Originality 30 pts New ideas, unique contribution, unpublished
Relevance 20 pts Aligns with global energy priorities, globally applicable
Methodology 20 pts Clear methods, valid data, appropriate framework
Analysis & Results 20 pts Meaningful conclusions, in-depth discussion
Scientific Writing 10 pts Systematic structure, standard language & formatting

Assessment Weight: 50% + 50%
